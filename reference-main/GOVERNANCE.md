## III. Core Operational Standards

### 1. Human-in-the-Loop (HITL) Requirement

- **Standard:** All implementations of the RFT architecture must maintain symbolic transparency and produce auditable, human-readable logs.
- **Mandate:** The deployment of fully autonomous, "black-box" systems using this resonance logic in critical infrastructure is **prohibited**. Systems must remain auditable by qualified human experts to ensure algorithmic accountability.
- **Retention Policy:** Audit logs for medical deployments must be retained for a minimum of **7 years** in compliance with HIPAA § 164.530.

### 2. Prohibition of Weaponized Dual-Use

- **Standard:** The RFT stack is strictly restricted from integration into lethal autonomous weapons systems (LAWS) or offensive cyber-warfare platforms.
- **Enforcement:** Contractual clauses in all commercial licenses forbid the use of the φ-basis in any hardware or software designed for non-defensive kinetic operations. Violations trigger automatic referral to the Department of Justice and OFAC under 18 U.S.C. § 2339.

### 3. Medical Advancement Fast-Track

- **Standard:** High-signal-fidelity applications (e.g., EEG, ECG, MRI, and neuro-prosthetics) are prioritized for deployment.
- **Mandate:** Qualified medical and biological research entities are granted a "Research-to-Clinical" fast-track. This protocol ensures that the 10⁻¹⁵ precision engine is accessible for life-saving applications without unnecessary administrative friction.
- **Regulatory Requirement:** FDA 510(k) or De Novo pathway clearance is required before any clinical diagnostic deployment.

### 4. Educational and Non-Profit Access

- **Standard:** Underlying mathematical frameworks and non-commercial research tools remain open to the global academic community.
- **Mandate:** Universities, public institutions, and certified non-profit research organizations are granted **royalty-free access** to documentation and academic-tier libraries under Creative Commons (CC-BY-NC).
- **Attribution Requirement:** All academic publications utilizing RFT-derived tools must cite: *USPTO Application No. 19/169,399, Luis M. Minier, 2026.*

### 5. Intellectual Property and Commercial Thresholds

- **Licensing:** Commercial use of the QuantoniumOS stack is strictly governed by a dual-license model (**AGPL + Non-Commercial**).
- **Revenue Threshold:** Commercial entities generating in excess of **$1,000,000 USD** in annual revenue from RFT-derived technologies must enter into a formal commercial agreement with the Minier family estate or its designated third-party trust within **90 days** of crossing that threshold.
- **Audit Rights:** The patent holder retains the right to audit any licensee's deployment on an annual basis to verify compliance with all standards stated herein.
